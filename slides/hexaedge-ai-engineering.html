  • Top passages injected as grounding

03 · Generation

The LLM writes the reading

A versioned prompt puts the result in the voice that fits the reader, in English or Chinese.

  • Practitioner: terms kept, each compiled into an observable market behaviour
  • First-timer: same result, plain language
  • Personal reading: one sustained metaphor, not a lecture
three voices, one engine

04 · Guardrails

Scanned before it ships

Every generation is checked against the compliance ban-list before it can reach a user.

  • One hit → retry with the violation named
  • Still dirty → killed, fallback ships
  • Nothing bad enters the cache
degrade, never leak
